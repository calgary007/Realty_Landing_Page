About Farhan

A clear plan.
Straight answers.

I’m a Toronto-based Sales Representative with Property.ca Inc., Brokerage. My job is simple: help you make a confident decision about buying, selling, or investing — without the pressure.

My background is in electrical and computer engineering. That training shapes how I work: I ask detailed questions, look closely at the numbers, and explain the trade-offs in plain language. You’ll always know what I’m seeing, what it means, and what comes next.

Whether you’re buying a first condo, selling a family home, or thinking through an investment, I’ll build a plan around your timeline, your budget, and what matters most to you.
